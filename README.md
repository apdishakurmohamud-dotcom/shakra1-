# Technical & Electronic - Dukaanka Qalabka Casriga ah & Elektaroonigga

Dukaan Online ah oo dhameystiran (E-Commerce Store) oo loogu talagalay iibinta qalabka farsamada iyo qalabka elektaroonigga ah, waxaana lagu dhisay **HTML5**, **CSS3**, iyo **JavaScript**.

---

## 🚀 Tilmaamaha & Astaamaha Dukaanka

1. **Magaca Dukaanka**: `Technical & Electronic` (Qalabka & Farsamada Casriga ah).
2. **Qeybaha Alaabta (Categories)**:
   - 💻 **Laptops & Kumbuyuutar**: MacBook Pro M3 Max, ASUS ROG Gaming, Samsung Odyssey 49" Monitor.
   - 📱 **Taleefanno & Tablets**: Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra, iPhone 15 Pro Max.
   - 🎧 **Qalabka Dhawaqa & Sound**: Sony WH-1000XM5, AirPods Pro 2.
   - ⚡ **Qeybaha Elektaroonigga & Tools**: Arduino Starter Kit, Raspberry Pi 5, Mitirka Korontada (Multimeter), Qalabka Alxanka (Soldering Station).
   - 🚁 **Qalabka Casriga ah (Gadgets)**: DJI Mini 4 Pro Drone, Apple Watch Ultra 2.
   - 🔌 **Agabka & Accessories**: Anker Power Bank 140W, Logitech MX Master 3S Mouse, SanDisk Extreme SSD 2TB.
3. **Adeeg Iibsi Dhameystiran (Full Checkout & Shopping Flow)**:
   - **Gaariga Iibsiga (Slide-Over Cart Drawer)**: Kudarid, tirada oo la kordhiyo/dhinco, tirtirid, iyo xisaabinta wadarta lacagta.
   - **Form-ka Dalabka (Checkout Form)**: Magaca, taleefanka, magaalada (Muqdisho, Hargeysa, Garoowe, Kismaayo, iwm), degmada iyo faahfaahinta gaarsiinta.
   - **Hababka Lacag-Bixinta Soomaalida & Caalamiga ah**:
     - 📱 **EVC Plus** (Hormuud)
     - 💳 **Zaad Service** (Telesom)
     - 📶 **Sahal Service** (Golis)
     - 💳 **Kaarka Bangiga** (Visa / Mastercard)
     - 💵 **Lacag bixi marka aad hesho** (Cash on Delivery)
   - **Rasiidka Dalabka (Invoice / Receipt)**: Nambarka tixraaca dalabka (#Order-ID), xogta macmiilka, alaabta la dalbaday, iyo badhanka daabacaadda rasiidka (`Print Receipt`).
4. **Adeegyada Dheeraadka ah**:
   - 🔍 **Live Search**: Raadin toos ah oo soo saarta alaabta isla marka aad qoreyso magaceeda.
   - 🏷️ **Sort & Filter**: Kala xulashada qiimaha (ugu jaban / ugu qaalisan), rating-ka ugu sareeya, ama magaca.
   - 👁️ **Quick View Modal**: Daawashada faahfaahinta farsamo (Specifications) ee alaab kasta.
   - ❤️ **Wishlist (Jecleysi)**: Meel lagu keydiyo alaabta aad xiisaynayso.
   - 🔔 **Toast Alerts**: Fariimo degdeg ah oo xaqiijinaya wax kasta oo la qabto.
   - 📱 **100% Mobile & Desktop Responsive**: U qaabeysan inuu si heersare ah ugu shaqeeyo kombuyuutarka, tablet-ka, iyo taleefannada gacanta.
